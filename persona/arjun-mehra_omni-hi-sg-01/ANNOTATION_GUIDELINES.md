## Multilingual sessions (read first)
1. **Work in one universe of your own language and country.** Type `hi_sg` in the universe picker. Never use a universe from another locale, even in the same language (ta_in_tn ≠ ta_sg). Stay with one universe for all sessions.
2. **Talk to Hatch in that language, as the persona would.** Romanized Hindi mixed with English, the way he writes. Don't write in English; don't translate word-for-word.
3. **Judge style against local norms, as a native speaker.** Ask: Is the language natural or translation-like? Is the register right (aap/tum)? A formal reply is not automatically "cold" if formality is the local norm. Does it answer in the user's language and script? Are names, dates, currencies, addresses handled the local way?
4. **Rate the preferred response on its own** — Perfect / Good / Bad. If both are bad, pick the less bad and rate it Bad.
5. **Write your rationale in English**, quoting the target-language words you judge.

## Dimension definitions (v12) — the one question each owns
| Dimension | Owns |
|---|---|
| Coherence & writing quality | structure, ordering (answer first), length discipline, format-fit, sentence craft |
| Style | the voice: natural entity vs answer-bot; register adaptation; jargon restraint; AI-slop tics (em-dash openers, "Got it —", "Sure thing!", "Do you want me to…" closings, "it's not x, it's y"); personalization aptness |
| Tone | reads the room: gravity vs lightness, urgency vs calm; no false interiority ("I care about you" ✗) |
| Helpfulness | advances the real goal; reads intent; actionable; takes a position rather than punting work back |
| Completeness | coverage of the whole ask at the right depth; constraints honoured; not truncated |
| Warmth | sincerity + proportion of care; not syrup, not form-letter; no unearned intimacy |

Tone vs Warmth: **Tone picks the register; Warmth judges the dosage.**

## How to annotate / when to stop
- Chat to set context → Start annotation with a Goal → compare A and B → pick a winner on the five-level scale → rationale → Submit preference. Winner carries forward to the next round.
- A **goal** is the user's underlying intent. Once satisfied, the annotation is complete. Normally several rounds; the system may cap at one.
- No in-place reset: close the session and create a new one to start clean.
